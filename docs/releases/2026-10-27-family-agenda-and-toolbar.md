# Family calendar agenda and compact page controls

At a Glance now uses the same calendar record readers, lineage deduplication and source-calendar membership as Family Calendar. It shows all Family entries for the viewed day without the personal responsibility filter, title-based grouping or six-item limit. All-day and spanning events are included; personal calendars remain selectable in the full calendar. Routine tasks remain in personal daily guidance.

Family Calendar opens to the household's current date in Agenda mode on every device. Calendar filters, custom date ranges and Month remain available after opening.

Every page now has one compact utility row for Back, Meeting History, sign-offs and Archive. Spiritual read-aloud joins the same row. Narrow screens scroll the utility row horizontally without widening the page. Active recording controls and errors remain available; redundant idle backup copy no longer occupies its own row. Read-aloud state remains owned by its content page. The redundant Overview tab is omitted for pillars with no subpages.

Validation uses synthetic records only: eight assigned Family events, a multi-day visit and a personal-calendar exclusion; switching calendars/views then reopening; custom/month boundaries; compact headers and working meeting/sign-off/archive controls across desktop, phone and both tablet orientations. Unit suite: 1,795 passing. Production build and source/bundle budgets pass. No household calendar or planning data was modified.
