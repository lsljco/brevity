# Daily rhythm, alignment and meeting continuity

- Alignment reuses Today’s dated meal images/macros, workout cards, chores and calendar agenda. The mobile footer no longer covers content.
- A session-level recorder persists across app navigation. Audio checkpoints are stored in IndexedDB every five seconds and backed up with transcripts to a private member archive. Stop finalizes capture; interrupted sessions retain saved portions. Browser/device termination may prevent the final unsaved portion from being captured. History supports playback and download.
- Today’s Household Management shows Anchor (04–08), Focus (08–12), Flex (12–16), Wind Down (16–20), and protected rest. My/Household scopes use explicit owners. Existing routines and chores remain authoritative; no automatic reassignment.
- Schedule A is transcribed from the August 4, 2026 Jenkins–Seay Schedule A–D Household Operating Guide. It is a reference until the member reviews its dated assignments through Action Mode. Exact source keys prevent repeated adoption of the same template items. Current locations/times must be checked during review. The original guide names Zion, not Isaiah; no template is silently attributed to an unnamed member.
- Routine/maintenance projections are excluded from Family Calendar. Explicit time blocks and major appointments remain. Existing chores, checklists, sign-off, version checks, Audit History and Undo remain in Household Operations.
- Personal web-push enrollment is separate from iCalendar. Each user adds Brevity to their iPhone Home Screen, opens it there, grants notification permission and enables reminders. Settings and test/disable controls are under Daily Rhythm. Signed-in identity is checked before saving the device.
- The once-per-minute server schedule reads current assignments, named fitness departures, exact-time daypart entries, routines and chores. Completed/deferred/cancelled entries are excluded. Quiet hours and lead time are per device. Missing exact times are not guessed. Current source reads prevent sending reminders based on an earlier schedule. A short delivery window avoids sending stale reminders after an outage.
- Web-push signing keys are generated once with an atomic create in a private, scoped server store. Only the public key is returned to the browser. Preview stores remain separate. The notification worker does not cache authenticated pages.
- Notifications are prompts, not guaranteed alarms. Actual iPhone delivery requires device permission, connectivity, and compatible Focus settings. Do not claim a successful real-device delivery from mocked browser or unit tests.
