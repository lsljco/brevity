# GOV-001 follow-through — October 5, 2026

This increment builds on the audit remediation and preserves the Seven Pillars, human ownership, existing member permissions, policy ratification, Action Mode review, audit, verification and conflict-aware Undo. Deployment does not ratify a policy, enable follow-up or opt a member into learning.

## Case closure and durable follow-up

Closing assistance is explicitly distinguished from completing or verifying the source obligation. System Health counts closed assistance whose source outcome remains unconfirmed. Such a closure no longer clears a dependency: the linked source must be completed or verified. Reviewed reopening and learning remain available.

The follow-up control offers reviewed delays of 1, 4, 24, 48, 72 or 168 hours through the existing snooze operation. It shows the saved resumption time in America/New_York. Source deadlines do not move.

The runner retains an operational case index independent of optional learning observations. It rotates the seven-date backlog window by last inspection, preserving older unresolved cases and retrying unavailable sources. Existing prompts for uninspected dates retain their original cooldown; source-unavailable prompts are withheld. The authenticated inbox rechecks source access and closure before displaying retained prompts. Operational identifiers remain separate from optional behavioral observations and immutable audit receipts.

## Cross-pillar coverage

Dated assignments already retain their pillar. Shared Health is now an additional native case source: needs scheduling, needs review, and appointments through seven days ahead, including unresolved past dates. A past appointment date is not inferred to be a missed appointment. Completed care is not described as clinically verified.

Health cases remain visible to the affected member and existing administrators. Preparation and reviewed follow-up reuse exact `health:care` resource versions. Automatic prompts go only to the affected member. Support requests, leadership routing and dependency links cannot expose Health details through this layer. Source navigation opens the existing Health screen. No appointment is booked and no clinical decision is made.

This does not claim native adapters for every specialized source in all Seven Pillars. Schedule conflicts still require named attendance and explicit times; a care date without a time cannot prove a calendar collision. External calendar/travel/resource conflicts remain outside that calculation.

## Adaptive measures

Repeated observations of the same case are deduplicated before outcome counts. Invalid states, future observations and invalid response times are excluded. Even-sized response samples use the mean of the two middle values for their median. Small samples are explicitly labeled as limited evidence. Original prompt styles, consent boundaries and source-date retention remain in force. These are descriptive workflow measures, not causal findings or character ratings.

## Verification

Regression coverage includes source-version conflicts, Health privacy and routing, source completion versus assistance closure, more than seven retained dates, outages without loss of the case index, duplicate/future observations and response-time arithmetic. The Health follow-up control is exercised across desktop, iPhone and both tablet layouts without executing a household action. Full repository tests, production build, source/bundle budgets and the broader browser suite remain release gates; passing synthetic browser tests is not a claim that every live third-party provider is healthy.
