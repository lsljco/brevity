# Home dashboard imagery

The hero uses the existing `public/imagery/home-lounge.webp`. Main meals use each saved meal's image, without guessing an image from its name. Missing images show an explicit fallback.

Two decorative snack illustrations were generated with the built-in image generator on 2026-11-13 and encoded as WebP without changing the composition. They are selected only for the exact saved snack IDs; nutrition remains sourced from the meal plan, not the images. A banana is decorative context, not an additional serving.

- `public/meal-images/home-banana-shake.webp`: “Create one square editorial food photograph for a luxury household meal dashboard: one glass containing a pale creamy banana protein shake, an unpeeled banana beside it, on a dark charcoal stone counter. Warm golden window light, restrained upscale food photography, close framing with the full glass visible, softly blurred dark kitchen background. No text, no logos, no packaging, no people. This image illustrates a banana protein shake serving.”
- `public/meal-images/home-envy-apple.webp`: “One square editorial food photograph for a luxury household meal dashboard. A single fresh red Envy apple with natural yellow streaks resting on a small white ceramic plate on dark charcoal stone. Warm golden window lighting, upscale restrained food photography, full apple and plate visible, softly blurred dark kitchen background. No text, no labels, no people, no other food. Photorealistic natural texture.”
