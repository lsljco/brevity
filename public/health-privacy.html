Brevity Health privacy

Updated September 30, 2026. This companion serves members of the Brevity household.

Your choice, per member

Sign in with your own Brevity account and confirm that the iPhone’s Health records belong to you. Choose steps, workouts, or both, then review the connection and Apple’s permission prompt. The app requests read access only. Apple Health originals are never edited.

What is stored

The service stores the latest snapshot of up to 30 daily step totals and workout summaries, your member identifier, a device identifier, sync timestamps, your goal, and your consent history. A new sync replaces the snapshot. If you stop syncing, the last snapshot remains until you disconnect. Workout counts merge overlapping recordings; window minutes can include pauses. No raw samples, routes, heart rate, medications, nutrition or clinical records are requested by this connection.

Private by default

Your summaries are accessible through your own member account. Application administrators do not receive them through household views unless you explicitly share selected daily totals. Hosting operators with infrastructure access may have access to stored data; this is not end-to-end encrypted storage. Brevity uses HTTPS and Netlify-hosted storage. The companion stores its session in the device Keychain and does not save your password.

Assistant and household sharing are separate

Allowing Ask Brevity access permits your own selected summaries to be included in requests to Brevity’s configured AI provider when you use the assistant. You can leave this off while syncing. Household sharing separately exposes the selected daily totals and step goal. This connection does not retrieve your personal ChatGPT conversations or ChatGPT Health history. Health data is not used here for advertising or tracking.

Revocation and deletion

Disconnect in the companion or Brevity’s Health Connections page to stop syncing, sharing and assistant access and remove the service’s synchronized summaries. Consent history is retained for accountability. Previously discussed information may remain in conversation history or copies already seen by others. Disconnecting cannot recall those copies. Turning a data type off removes that type from stored summaries. Apple Health originals remain on your iPhone. You may also revoke permissions in Apple Health; Apple does not tell Brevity whether unreadable data means denied access or no records.

Freshness and limits

Background delivery is controlled by iOS. Open the companion and choose Sync now for a fresh reading. Daily boundaries use America/New_York. Unknown is not zero; today’s totals are partial. This feature is not continuous medical monitoring, diagnosis or an emergency service. No treatment decisions should be made from these totals alone.

Questions

Contact your Brevity household administrator through your usual household contact method. You can manage your choices in Brevity → Health & Nutrition → Health Connections.
